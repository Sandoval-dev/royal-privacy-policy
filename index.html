English

1. App and contact

Royal is a science-fiction mobile game. For privacy inquiries, contact osmankilic10@gmail.com. This policy was updated on 16 October 2026.

2. Game data stored on your device

Royal stores game progress, captains, discoveries, achievements, in-game credits, cargo, daily reward history, ad reward receipts and preferences in local app storage. Reminder preferences, the last played/reminder dates, and your response to a review invitation may also be stored locally. Royal has no player account system or developer-operated cloud save server. If optional local gameplay measurements are enabled, limited voyage, decision, minigame and advertising events may be logged on your device; the game does not use a separate analytics upload server for this log.

3. Advertising and third-party processing

Royal uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent choices. Google advertising technologies may process or share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising/device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. This is distinct from Royal operating its own player database. Advertising privacy choices are offered when required in your region. Rewarded ads are optional. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

4. Connectivity and time checks

The app may contact HTTPS endpoints such as connectivitycheck.gstatic.com and cp.cloudflare.com to verify internet access. Daily reward time checks may use timeapi.io and Google HTTPS responses. Your IP address and standard request metadata may be visible to those providers; game saves and captain names are not added to these requests. A daily reward claim may be deferred if verification fails. Client-side verification without an authoritative game server cannot prevent every kind of clock or local save manipulation.

5. Notifications and ratings

If you opt in, Android may schedule a local daily reminder. You may decline notification permission or change it later in device settings. Royal does not request your contacts, precise location or a remote push token for these reminders. A rating invitation may open Google Play or the platform review interface; Royal cannot read your review. The prompt status and deferral date may be stored locally.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game records may remain until you clear app data or uninstall Royal. Some data may be retained across device transfers or backups depending on Android backup settings. Clearing data may erase progress and in-game earnings. Google and other network providers follow their own retention and deletion rules; clearing local app data does not automatically erase their records.

7. Security and your rights

Network requests use HTTPS. Local game saves are not represented as encrypted or tamper-proof storage. For access, correction or deletion questions, contact osmankilic10@gmail.com. Since there is no player account, we cannot remotely access saves stored on your device. Rights under applicable law remain available.

8. Age audience and changes

Royal is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Advertising and consent features are subject to applicable platform rules and local law. We update this policy when relevant app functionality or data flows change; the latest revision date appears on this page.
