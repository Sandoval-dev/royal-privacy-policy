Gameplay and local records

Royal stores progress, captains, discoveries, achievements, credits, cargo, daily supply history, advertising reward receipts, and settings on your device. This version has no account system or Royal-operated game-save server. Android app storage also holds your reminder preference, last played date, and last reminder date. Your response to a review invitation and its deferral time are stored locally. These records support continuity, prevent duplicate rewards, and reduce unwanted prompts.

If you enable optional gameplay measurements, a bounded local log records voyage, decision, planet, mini-game, ending, and advertising events. The inspected source contains no upload connection to an analytics server. Disabling this setting clears that local log; it does not stop the advertising provider’s processing.

Advertising and consent

Royal uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google’s SDK may process and share approximate location derived from IP, advertising/device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent abuse. Actual processing depends on the SDK version, consent, age treatment, and advertising configuration. Royal’s local measurement setting is separate from these SDK choices.

Google’s consent flow is used where required. When required by the provider, the in-game Ad privacy choices control lets you change your selections. Rewarded ads are optional. Notification permission is a separate choice.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance. Adding mediation partners requires a fresh assessment of this policy.

Connectivity and time verification

At launch and while the game is in the foreground, HTTPS connectivity checks contact Google’s connectivitycheck.gstatic.com and Cloudflare’s cp.cloudflare.com endpoints. Daily supplies obtain UTC time from timeapi.io and compare it with the date on an independent Google HTTPS response. Network requests disclose your IP and ordinary connection/request metadata to those providers. Royal does not add your player name, save contents, or advertising identifier to these requests. If time verification fails, new daily payouts wait; existing rewards are preserved.

These are client-side checks without an authoritative game server. They cannot completely prevent local file editing, reinstall abuse, or all clock/time-zone manipulation. TimeAPI’s terms and availability require review before publication: timeapi.io. Connectivity verification is independent of advertising availability. expo-network listens only for on-device network-state changes; Royal does not use it to query IP addresses, Wi-Fi names, or location. Confirmed in-game connection loss pauses the current screen with a warning; it does not erase progress.

Reminders and reviews

If you choose to enable reminders, Android notification permission is requested. One inexact local alarm is scheduled for 20:00; reminders stay quiet on days you play. Android battery restrictions can delay or prevent delivery. This feature requests no push token, remote messaging service, location, or contacts access. Turning it off in settings cancels the pending alarm. You can also use Android’s notification settings.

The Review option opens the configured, actual Google Play listing. Royal does not read your review and offers no reward for a positive rating. Your store activity is governed by Google’s terms.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local records can remain until you clear application data or uninstall. Android backup/restore behavior depends on the final local project configuration; the developer must confirm the release’s backup policy. Clearing data can permanently erase progress and earned in-game currency. Third-party providers, including Google, apply their own retention/deletion rules. Removing local data does not automatically delete third-party records.

You may contact osmankilic10@gmail.com with access, correction, or deletion questions. We will take account of the fact that this version has no player account and we cannot remotely access your local save. Rights depend on applicable law. Network requests use HTTPS; the local save is not an encrypted, tamper-proof vault, and absolute security is not promised.
